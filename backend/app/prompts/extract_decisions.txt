You extract decisions from one chunk of a meeting transcript.

A decision is a choice the group settled on and intends to act on. Something
was open, and by the end of the exchange it is closed.

{context}

TRANSCRIPT CHUNK
The lines below are the only text you may quote. The context block above is
background and must never appear in a quote.

{chunk}

WHAT IS NOT A DECISION. Read this list twice before answering.

1. Anything proposed and then put off. If anyone says to wait, defer, park it,
   revisit it, come back to it later, or gather more information first, then
   NOTHING WAS DECIDED, however much preference was voiced beforehand.
   "I like Amplitude better, but let's defer this until we have the Q3 numbers"
   is not a decision to use Amplitude. It is not a decision at all.
   This is the single most common mistake. When in doubt, leave it out.

2. A choice still being argued when the chunk ends.

3. A preference or an opinion. "I'd go with Postgres" is a preference. It
   becomes a decision only when the group settles on it.

4. A commitment to do work. "I'll take the booking engine" is an action item,
   not a decision. It is extracted elsewhere.

5. A statement of existing fact. "We use Python" describes what is already
   true and decides nothing.

6. A recap. When someone closes a meeting by listing what was agreed, those
   are the same decisions made earlier, not new ones. Extract a decision from
   the moment it was settled, not from the moment it was repeated back.

A DEFERRAL IS SOMETIMES ITSELF A DECISION, and a different one. If the group
settles on a substitute while postponing the real choice, the substitute is the
decision. "Let's not waste time naming it now, call it HotelOS as a working
name" decides to use a working name. It does not decide the permanent name.
Record what was settled, never what was postponed.

RULES:

1. verbatim_quote must be copied character for character from the TRANSCRIPT
   CHUNK above. Do not tidy it, do not fix grammar, do not include the
   "[00:00:00] Name:" prefix. Quote the words where the choice was settled,
   not where it was first floated. A quote that is not an exact substring of
   the chunk will be rejected.

2. what_was_decided is the choice, stated plainly, under 25 words.

3. who_stated_it is the person who articulated the settled decision, named
   exactly as they appear in the participant list. If nobody clearly did,
   output exactly UNSPECIFIED.

4. stated_rationale is the reason the transcript gives, in your own words. If
   no reason was given, output exactly UNSPECIFIED. Never supply a reason that
   sounds plausible but was not said.

5. alternatives_discussed lists the other options the transcript actually
   mentions. An empty list is correct when none were.

6. confidence is your honest probability from 0.0 to 1.0 that this was settled
   rather than merely discussed. Use a low value when the chunk ends before
   anyone agreed. Do not return the same value for every item.

If the chunk contains no decisions, return an empty list. An empty list is a
correct answer and is far better than recording something that was deferred.
