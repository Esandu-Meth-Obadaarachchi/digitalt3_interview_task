---
You extract risks and blockers from one chunk of a meeting transcript.

A risk is something that could go wrong and would have consequences. A blocker
is something already stopping work. Both belong in your output.

{context}

TRANSCRIPT CHUNK
The lines below are the only text you may quote. The context block above is
background and must never appear in a quote.

{chunk}

WHAT IS NOT A RISK:

1. Work that is merely unfinished. "The pipeline is sixty percent done" is
   progress, not a risk. It becomes one when somebody says it might not land.

2. A difficulty already resolved. "The credentials expired and I had to wait
   on IT, that's done now" is a story about a past blocker, not a live one.

3. A complaint with no consequence. Somebody disliking a tool is not a risk
   unless the transcript says what it threatens.

4. A task. "We need to write more tests" is an action item. "We have no test
   coverage on the aggregation logic and if the numbers are wrong the client
   loses trust" is a risk, because it names what could go wrong and what
   follows.

SEVERITY. This is the part that gets checked, so read it carefully.

Severity must be defensible from the quote alone. Somebody reading only the
verbatim quote, with no other context, must be able to see why you chose it.
If the quote does not support the level, choose the lower one.

  high    the transcript states a serious consequence: a missed deadline, a
          delayed go-live, lost client trust, a compliance or legal problem,
          or work that cannot proceed at all
  medium  a real problem with a stated impact that is not severe, or a serious
          problem whose consequence the transcript leaves vague
  low     a concern raised with no stated consequence

Never use high because the topic sounds serious. Use it because the speaker
said what would happen.

RULES:

1. verbatim_quote must be copied character for character from the TRANSCRIPT
   CHUNK above. Do not tidy it, do not fix grammar, do not include the
   "[00:00:00] Name:" prefix. Quote the words that state both the problem and
   its consequence where possible, because the severity has to be defensible
   from this quote alone. A quote that is not an exact substring of the chunk
   will be rejected.

2. description states the problem and its consequence, under 40 words.

3. affected_area is the part of the work at stake: a module, a deadline, a
   deliverable. If the transcript does not make it clear, output exactly
   UNSPECIFIED.

4. owner is the person accountable for the risk, if the transcript names one.
   Raising a risk is not owning it. Somebody flagging a problem is not
   automatically responsible for it. When no owner is named, output exactly
   UNSPECIFIED. Never infer an owner from who happened to speak.

5. confidence is your honest probability from 0.0 to 1.0 that this is a real
   risk rather than ordinary discussion. Do not return the same value for
   every item.

If the chunk contains no risks or blockers, return an empty list. An empty list
is a correct answer.
